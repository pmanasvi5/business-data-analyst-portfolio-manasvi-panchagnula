## Business Problem

Help public-service operations staff understand workload, closure performance, and open-request aging within a defined cohort.

## Objective

Build a reproducible model that reports closure and backlog measures at a fixed cutoff without misleading open-case durations.

## Data

A frozen, limited NYC 311 Open Data cohort with documented filters, dates, fields, exclusions, and privacy treatment.

## Process

### 1. Define
Clarify the audience, decision, scope, measures, and success criteria.

### 2. Prepare
Collect, clean, document, and validate the planned inputs.

### 3. Analyze
Apply the project methods and independently check the important calculations.

### 4. Visualize and Communicate
Build a reviewable output and connect verified evidence to recommendations.
