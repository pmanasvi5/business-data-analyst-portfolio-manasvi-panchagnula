## Before Publishing

- Remove your full street address and other unnecessary personal information.
- Confirm that your email address, LinkedIn URL, experience, and dates are current.
- Use a text-based PDF so applicant tracking systems can read it.
- Keep the filename professional and replace the file whenever you update your résumé.
