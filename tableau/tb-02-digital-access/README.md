## Business Problem

Help a market-research team compare internet access across countries and understand its relationship with economic context.

## Objective

Build a transparent country comparison that supports research without presenting a definitive market-entry ranking.

## Data

World Bank internet-use and constant-2015-US-dollar GDP-per-capita indicators for a documented country set and period.
